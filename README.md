# Jailbreak Prompts

A collection of jailbreak prompts for AIs.

## Notes

- Results vary by model and change over time as models are updated.
- Prompts tend to work more often on smaller or less capable models.
- They are less likely to work on stronger models, or on systems with search and deep research modes.
